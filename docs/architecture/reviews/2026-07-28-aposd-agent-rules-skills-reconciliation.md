## 5. Material debate and resolution

### 5.1 Register the primary source or evaluate the treatment first

The architecture-fit reviewer considered adding a verified APOSD source family
after source/provenance reconciliation because the source registry is itself
nonconformant research data. The evidence and adversarial reviewers objected
that changing the exact nine-family denominator would immediately cascade
through profiles, counts, digests, compiler output, generated contracts, and
coverage claims before incremental value was known.

**Reconciled split:** the exhaustive recursive inventory includes the PDF
because it is already inside the declared live corpus root. The current
definition artifacts therefore record APOSD as family ten and expose four
primary-source practices in the 38-practice design profile. This review reports
that state; it is not its authority source.

The evidence/adversarial caution still governs the separate question of
third-party instructions. No ciembor or Matt payload and no seven-question
treatment is registered or executable. Incremental treatment effectiveness
remains unevaluated, all generated unresolved source-family dispositions
remain `UNKNOWN`, and the design profile cannot seal runtime application.

### 5.2 Whole rule packs or a small adapted lens

The rule repository provides compact instructions and Matt's skills provide a
usable discovery workflow. Both also carry source-provenance, workflow,
vocabulary, test, automation, and authority assumptions that do not fit Ranex
as-is.

**Resolution:** compare the current Ranex baseline only with the seven-question
Ranex synthesis. Do not test or activate a raw whole-book `OBEY` instruction as
the primary candidate, and do not install or run the third-party skills.

### 5.3 Numeric score or noncompensating evidence

The supplied analyses and source materials include scores, maturity language,
or count/ratio heuristics. Those measures can hide a mandatory failure and
cannot prove qualitative architecture judgment.

**Resolution:** prohibit an aggregate APOSD score. Evaluate trigger
correctness, findings, prohibited conclusions, exact-subject accuracy,
authority compatibility, abstraction/public-surface growth, test/failure
evidence, rework, cost, and human burden separately. `UNKNOWN` does not average
into `PASS`.

### 5.4 Run immediately or remain DRAFT

A public 12-task development set and a paired protocol now exist, but the
runtime prerequisites do not:

- authenticated `process_assurance` owner and registration decision;
- exact route/model/harness/tool/prompt/output locks;
- hidden anchors and leakage isolation;
- calibrated independent verifier and human rubric;
- seed schedule, replication count, local variance, minimum detectable effect,
  and acceptance margin;
- immutable equal-budget binding and analysis code; and
- qualified runtime producers.

The current `FleetExperimentV1` schema also has two blocking design defects:
`equal_budget_profile` is a closed object with no permitted fields, while
`result_ref` is a required non-null string before a result exists. Its arm,
stratum, lock, topology, seed, and evidence item shapes are open, and the task
population has a digest but no required manifest reference.

**Resolution:** keep the artifact `DRAFT`. Running it now would violate the
experiment's own preregistration and authority rules and could not support an
effectiveness or activation decision.
